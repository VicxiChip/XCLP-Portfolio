About Me

Hello! I'm Xhiro Chiyam L. Pagnas — a self‑taught 3D artist specializing in Blender since 2019, with a focus on modeling and shading. In 2024, I began exploring animation and editing, expanding my creative toolkit.

My passion lies in creating stylized 3D art, experimenting with lighting, and bringing ideas to life through animation. I enjoy blending technical precision with artistic creativity to produce unique digital works.

Beyond art, I’m continuously learning new tools and techniques to refine my craft and share my vision with others.
